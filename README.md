# Royal Sweets Platform

Create a fully functional, high-end, highly attractive e-commerce store and online ordering platform for 'AL MALEK SWEETS' (حلويات الملك), a premier Syrian sweet and custom cake shop based in Utrecht, Netherlands.

1. BRANDING & VISUAL IDENTITY:
- Color Palette: Rich Royal Dark Brown (#2D1E18 / #3C241B), Metallic Warm Gold accents (#D4AF37 / #FFD700), and Cream/Ivory backgrounds (#FAF7F2).
- Header & Logo Design: Render a dynamic 3D golden crown icon at the top, accompanied by 'AL MALEK' in bold golden uppercase serif font, and 'حلويات الملك' underneath in elegant golden Arabic calligraphy.
- Design Aesthetics: Ultra-luxurious, modern, smooth animations, interactive card hover effects, and warm premium food-photography presentation.

2. MULTILINGUAL & RTL SUPPORT:
- Header Language Switcher: Full dynamic toggle between Dutch (NL), Arabic (AR), and English (EN).
- Full Arabic RTL Support: Ensure the UI layout flips smoothly to Right-to-Left when Arabic is selected.

3. CORE E-COMMERCE & ORDERING FEATURES:
- Interactive Menu Categories: Pre-populate the menu with mouth-watering items, localized titles, real descriptions, prices, and high-quality image placeholders:
  * Baklava & Dry Pastries: Mixed Syrian Baklava Box, Kol W Shkor (Cashew Fingers), Mabroume with Pistachio, Warbat with Cream, Barazek & Maamoul Box.
  * Syrupy & Warm Desserts: Nabulsi Kunafa (Fine/Coarse), Syrian Basbousa / Namoora with Almonds, Halawet El Jibn, Mafroukeh with Cream.
  * Cakes & Celebration Specials: Pistachio Cream Cake, Royal Chocolate Layer Cake.
- Custom Cake Reservation System: A dedicated interactive booking modal allowing users to customize custom cakes for weddings and birthdays (select size, layers, flavors, upload a reference design photo, pick pickup/delivery date & time).
- Shopping Cart & Checkout: Full cart management with option selection for Direct Store Pickup or Home Delivery.

4. STORE INFORMATION & CONTACT (PRE-FILLED):
- Address: Amsterdamsestraatweg 101, 3513 AC Utrecht

- Email: almaleksweets7@gmail.com
- Social Links: Direct integration buttons for Facebook (Almalek Sweets) and Instagram.
- Contact Features: Floating WhatsApp Direct Order button on bottom right, and embedded Google Maps iframe for store location.

5. POLISH & INTERACTION EFFECTS:
- Glowing gold CTA buttons, subtle scroll-reveal animations for menu items, floating cart preview, and smooth interactive mobile drawer menu.
